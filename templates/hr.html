Evidence base
Check-in frequency — managers often underestimate the value of regular contact in the first 90 days. Infrequent check-ins correlate with lower re-entry confidence and higher attrition risk.
Meeting inclusion — exclusion from planning meetings is one of the most commonly reported re-entry barriers, often driven by unconscious assumptions about availability and engagement.
Milestone visibility — completing visible milestones supports identity reactivation and confidence recovery, particularly in weeks 3–6 of return.
