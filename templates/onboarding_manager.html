What good support looks like

This is not a training module. It is a two-minute read that gives you the context behind what Hearth will ask you to do.

Women returning to work after a career break often experience a gap that is structural, not personal. Skills can feel rusty. Team relationships need rebuilding. Confidence in professional identity takes time to recover. None of this is a reflection of capability.

The research consistently shows that one of the most powerful things a manager can do is signal belief. Acknowledging the return directly, including the returning employee in decisions, and noticing their contributions early on makes a measurable difference to how quickly and confidently they re-establish themselves.

Hearth will send you a small number of suggested actions over the next 90 days. These are not mandatory — they are prompts based on what the evidence says works. You are also encouraged to reach out to Sarah directly whenever it feels right.
